## What this does not establish

The safe policy assumes publication, in-flight draining, invalidation service,
and completion contracts. A counterexample in a mutant establishes a failure
of that model, not an executable Oak or ARM counterexample. Both traversals
share the transition relation. Bounds and omitted behaviors are documented
in the [README](../README.md).

No Rust-to-CAT trace relation exists yet. The existing official Herd/CAT cases
were not rerun for this batch (`herd7` was unavailable in this environment).
Connecting the protocol to actual architectural events and proving the
required implementation/refinement contracts remain open. These counts are
not performance measurements or evidence of Apple-Silicon optimality.
